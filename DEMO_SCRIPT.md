# 🎬 TimeWorth — 3-Minute Demo Walkthrough Script

This script is designed for a fast-paced, high-impact demo or hackathon presentation of **TimeWorth**.

---

## 🎯 The Hook (0:00 – 0:30)

> *"When you look at a price tag — say ₹4,999 for sneakers or ₹450 for Friday night delivery — your brain sees arbitrary numbers. Money feels abstract. But your time on this planet is finite and irreplaceable.*
>
> *TimeWorth changes the question from **'Can I afford this?'** to **'How many hours of my life am I willing to trade for this?'** — and then shows what that same time could buy you in future freedom."*

---

## 🏃‍♂️ Step-by-Step Demo Flow

### 1. The Reality Check: Hero & Presets (0:30 – 1:00)
1. **Show the Clean Dark UI:** Point out the dark mode theme, the electric violet/cyan accents, and the income chip in the top bar (`₹50,000/mo · ₹284/hr`).
2. **Click Preset 1 (₹4,999 sneakers):**
   - Watch the animated count-up land on **17 hours 36 minutes**.
   - Point out the human-scale pill: *"That’s equivalent to 2.2 full 8-hour work shifts!"*
   - Ask the audience: *"Would you give up your entire Tuesday and Wednesday at work just for these shoes?"*
3. **Change to a larger purchase (e.g. ₹1,20,000 iPhone):**
   - Numbers update instantly: **422 hours (52.8 work days / 2.4 months of full-time work)**.

### 2. The Opportunity Cost: Freedom Growth Slider (1:00 – 1:40)
1. **Scroll down to the Freedom Growth section:**
   - Emphasize the Indian mutual fund baseline: **12% historical annualized returns**.
2. **Move the Year Slider from 10 to 20 years:**
   - Show how the ₹4,999 sneakers grow into **₹48,222**.
   - Point out the translation back into freedom: **170 hours (21 full days of retirement freedom)**.
3. **Hover on the AreaChart:**
   - Show the flat red/gray "money spent" line vs. the glowing green exponential "freedom if invested" curve.
   - Quote: *"You're not just trading 2 days of work today; you're trading 3 weeks of early retirement tomorrow."*

### 3. The AI Verdict Card (1:40 – 2:10)
1. **Click "Get AI Verdict":**
   - Watch the streaming response arrive via Gemini AI (`gemini-2.5-flash`).
   - Notice the tone: Witty, constructive, and realistic — never preachy.
   - Look at the **Cheaper Alternative**: e.g., *"Wait 48 hours and check if your existing sneakers still have 500km in them, or consider quality alternatives at ₹1,999 to bank 10 hours of your life back."*
2. **Type a quick follow-up:** e.g., *"What if I wear them every single day?"*
   - AI recalculates the cost-per-wear in minutes of life.

### 4. Smart Natural Language Recurring Expenses (2:10 – 2:35)
1. **Scroll to the Smart Input section:**
2. **Type:** `"swiggy 400 twice a week"`
   - Watch it parse instantly into:
     - Amount: **₹400**
     - Frequency: **twice a week (104 times/year)**
     - Category: **🍔 Food**
3. **Inspect the recurring cost cards:**
   - Annual cost: **₹41,600 / year (146 hours of work)**
   - 10-Year Opportunity Cost: **₹7.3 Lakhs (2,570 hours of freedom)**
4. **Click "Add to Cart":**
   - Item drops into the cart drawer.

### 5. Multi-Item Cart & Social Share Card (2:35 – 3:00)
1. **Open the Cart Drawer:**
   - Shows both individual items and the cumulative life-hours required.
   - Demo the **Undo button** by removing and restoring an item.
2. **Click "Share Card":**
   - High-res 1:1 modal opens with an Instagram-ready gradient graphic.
   - Notice the **Privacy Guarantee**: It shows the item, the price, and the life-hours, but *never reveals your personal salary*.
   - Tap **"Copy Link" / "Save Card Image"**.
3. **Switch Language (Top Bar):**
   - Click the language badge to toggle to **தமிழ் (Tamil)**.
   - Entire interface switches seamlessly with natural Tamil vernacular.

## 🏆 Closing Pitch
> *"TimeWorth doesn't tell people to stop spending. It gives them the clarity to spend on what truly matters to them by measuring the only currency that cannot be refunded: their time."*
